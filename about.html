I'm studying a BSc in Computer Science at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in Budapest, Hungary, graduating in 2027.

Alongside my studies I work as a Software Engineering Intern on CodeChecker, an open-source static analysis platform. The work is issue-driven: reading an unfamiliar codebase, reproducing bugs, and investigating problems in the web GUI, product configuration, and report parsing.

The areas I want to grow in are software engineering, Linux, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, and AI engineering. Whatever I build, I care about it being useful, maintainable, and technically sound, and I review the code AI assistants write for me as carefully as my own.
